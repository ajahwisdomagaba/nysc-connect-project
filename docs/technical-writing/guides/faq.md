## 1\. General / About the Platform

***What is NYSC Connect?***

NYSC Connect is a location-based settlement and information platform for newly deployed Corps Members. It helps you find accommodation and organized local information for the place you have been posted to.

***Who is NYSC Connect for?***

Mainly Corps Members settling into an unfamiliar State or LGA. Landlords and housing agents are secondary users: once verified, they can submit accommodation listings, which an admin must approve before they appear.

***Is NYSC Connect a property marketplace?***

No. Accommodation is the main use case, but the platform does not include booking, payment, escrow, or chat.

## 2\. Getting Started / Registration and Onboarding

***How do I create an account?***

Register with your name, phone number, email address, and password, then choose your State and LGA. Your phone number and email must not already be registered, and the phone number must have at least 10 characters.

***How do I log in?***

Log in with your phone number and password. Your email address is not used to log in.

***Do I need to know my PPA to register?***

No. PPA is optional. You can skip it and add it later. Adding it lets listings show their proximity to your PPA.

***What happens after I register?***

You go to a home screen with accommodation and local information for your selected State and LGA.

***Can I change my State, LGA, or PPA later?***

Yes. You can update your location details after registration.

## 3\. Accommodation and Housing

***What information will I see on a listing?***

Each listing shows the property type, the annual rent, the caution fee, the agency fee (if any), the address, proximity to your PPA, photos (up to three), property information, the source of the information, and the date it was last updated. Some listings also show contact details supplied with the listing.

***Can I filter and sort listings?***

Yes. You can filter by State, LGA and price range. You can sort by newest, lowest price or highest price.

***How is the distance to my PPA shown?***

As text supplied with the listing, such as "approximately 7 minutes by bus". It is not a calculated GPS distance.

***How do I know if a listing can be trusted?***

Check the source and the last-updated date. Listings not updated within 30 days show "may be outdated". Only listings approved by an admin appear on the public feed. Admin approval is a content check, not a guarantee that every detail is accurate, and the app does not show a verified badge. Always confirm details yourself before making any payment.

***Can I contact the landlord or book through the app?***

There is no in-app booking, payment, or chat in this release. Where a listing includes contact details, you can use them directly.

***Can I save a listing?***

Yes. Log in, then save the listing. Saved items are still available after you log in again.

***What do I do if I suspect a listing is a scam?***

Use the report option on the listing, choose a reason (Outdated, Incorrect, Misleading, or Suspicious), and add details if you wish. Your report goes to an admin for review.

***Why do I see "No accommodation listings found for this location yet"?***

There are no approved listings for that location at the moment. This is expected behaviour, not an error. Listings are added location by location, so content may not be available for every LGA yet.

## 5\. Local Guide and Discovery

***What local information is available?***

The Local Guide has six categories: Transport, PPA Information, Health, Security and Safety, Food, and Essential Services. Entries are filtered to your State and LGA.

***Do I need an account to use the Local Guide?***

Yes. You need to be logged in.

***Can I save or report a Local Guide entry?***

Yes. You can save an entry for later, and you can report one that looks outdated, incorrect, misleading, or suspicious.

***What if there is no Local Guide information for my LGA?***

You will see a message that information for your LGA is not available yet. This is expected behaviour. Local Guide content is added location by location, so it is not guaranteed for every LGA.

## 7\. For Landlords / Agents

***Can I list my property on NYSC Connect?***

Yes, in a controlled way. Register as a landlord or agent, send your verification documents, wait for an admin to verify your account, then submit your listing. Every listing stays pending until an admin approves it. Nothing is published automatically.

For the full process, see the Guide for Landlords and Agents.

***Why do I have to verify my identity?***

To protect Corps Members from fake listings and scams, and to keep the listings on the platform trustworthy. Verification means an admin has reviewed the verification documents you sent: your identity document and your proof of authority. It is not a legal ruling on who owns a property.

***What documents are accepted?***

Send an identity document: NIN, voter's card (PVC), driver's licence or international passport. Also send proof of authority. Landlords send a utility bill (with the prepaid meter number) or proof of ownership. Agents send a landlord approval letter or an agency registration document. You also agree not to charge any inspection or mobilization fee.

***Is verification automatic?***

No. An admin reviews your documents by hand. There is no automatic scanning and no check against any government database in this release.

***If my account is verified, is my property approved?***

No. Account verification and listing approval are two separate checks. Verification only lets you submit listings. An admin must still approve each listing before it is published.

***What documents do I need to list a property?***

You send your identity and proof of authority once, when you apply for account verification. After that you can submit many listings, and each listing is approved separately. You do not attach documents again for each listing.

***What happens if my verification is rejected?***

You will see the reason. You cannot post listings until you are verified. Correct your documents and submit them again. Your status returns to pending and an admin reviews it again.

***What happens if my listing is rejected?***

A rejected listing is not published, and you can see the admin's reason. Admins approve or reject listings, so a rejected listing is not sent back for editing. Correct the details and submit a new listing. There is no appeal process in this release.

***Why can't I post a listing yet?***

You will see one of these messages. "Submit identity and authority documents before posting a listing" means you have not sent your documents. "Your account verification is still pending admin review" means an admin has not finished checking them. "Your account verification was rejected. Update your documents and submit again" means you need to send corrected documents.

***Why is my listing not showing?***

New listings stay pending until an admin approves them. Rejected listings are not public. If you edit an approved listing, it goes back to pending until an admin approves it again.

## 9\. Account, Privacy and Security

***What account types exist?***

Three roles: Corps Member, Landlord/Agent (a landlord or a housing agent), and Admin. Admin accounts are internal and cannot be created through registration.

***How is my password protected?***

The platform is designed to store passwords securely. You log in with your phone number and password.

***Who can see my verification documents?***

Your verification documents are stored securely and are never shown publicly.

## 10\. Trust, Safety and Moderation

***How does NYSC Connect help me avoid scams?***

Every listing shows its source and last-updated date, only admin-approved listings are published, and you can report any listing or Local Guide entry that looks wrong.

***What reasons can I choose when I report something?***

Outdated, Incorrect, Misleading, or Suspicious. You can add optional details.

***What happens after I submit a report?***

Your report is saved and added to an admin review queue. An admin reviews it and can resolve it, dismiss it, or take moderation action on the item. A confirmed scam report hides the listing. Reports are not silently dropped.

## 11\. Technical Support / Troubleshooting

***The app will not load or show a network error. What should I do?***

Check your internet connection and try again. The app shows a network message when it cannot reach the server. On the web app, an offline banner appears when your connection drops, and screens that load data show a Retry button. The app also tries again by itself when your connection returns.

***I cannot log in.***

Check your phone number and password. If either is wrong, you will see "Invalid credentials". If a field is empty, you will see "Please provide phone number and password".

***I was logged out unexpectedly.***

Your login session expires after a set time. Log in again with your phone number and password. On the web app, you are taken back to the onboarding screen.

***I cannot register.***

Check that your name, phone number, email, password, State, and LGA are filled in. The phone number and email must not already be registered.

***I see an empty screen for accommodation or the Local Guide.***

This usually means no content has been added for your location yet. See the questions in sections 3 and 5\.
