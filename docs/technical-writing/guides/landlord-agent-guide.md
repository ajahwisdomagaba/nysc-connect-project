# NYSC Connect: Guide for Landlords and Agents

*Version 1.0 (MVP)*

## About this guide

NYSC Connect helps Corps Members find accommodation and local information for the State and LGA they are posted to. Landlords and housing agents can add accommodation listings, but only in a controlled way. First your account must be verified. Then an admin must approve each listing before Corps Members can see it.

This guide explains each step, which documents you need, and what happens if something is rejected.

## How it works: two separate approvals

Account verification and listing approval are two different checks. Passing the first does not pass the second.

|  | 1\. Account verification | 2\. Listing approval |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| What is checked | Who you are, and that you are allowed to list property | Each property you submit |
| What you send | Verification documents: an ID and proof of authority | The property details and up to three photos |
| Who checks | An admin, by hand | An admin, by hand |
| Possible results | Verified or Verification Rejected | Approved or Rejected |
| What it unlocks | The ability to submit listings | Your listing becomes visible to Corps Members |

**The full path:** Register, send your documents, admin verifies your account, submit a listing, admin reviews the listing, listing is approved and published.

## Why we ask you to verify your identity

Many Corps Members arrive in a new place with no local contacts, and fake listings and scams are a real risk. Checking who is posting protects Corps Members. It also protects honest landlords and agents, because Corps Members can use the platform with more confidence.

Verification means an admin has reviewed the verification documents you sent: your identity document and your proof of authority. It is not a legal ruling on who owns a property. NYSC Connect does not check ownership beyond the documents you send and the admin review. Your documents are stored securely and are never shown publicly. NYSC Connect does not show a verified badge on profiles or listings.

### Step 1: Create your account

Register as a landlord or as an agent. You will need:

* Full name

* Phone number (at least 10 characters, not already registered)

* Email address (not already registered)

* Password

* Account type: Landlord or Agent

* State and LGA where you operate

After you register, your account is marked Not submitted. You cannot post a listing yet. You log in with your phone number and password.

### Step 2: Verify your account

Send the documents below. Upload each one as a clear image, one image per document, up to 5 MB each. Then submit them together.

| Document | Landlord | Agent |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Identity document (accepted types) | National ID (NIN), voter's card (PVC), driver's licence, or international passport | National ID (NIN), voter's card (PVC), driver's licence, or international passport |
| Proof of authority | A utility bill (you must also give your prepaid meter number) or proof of ownership | A landlord approval (authorization) letter or an agency registration document |
| Agreement | You agree not to charge any inspection or mobilization fee | You agree not to charge any inspection or mobilization fee |

An admin checks your documents by hand. There is no automatic scanning, and there is no check against any government database in this release.

| Your account status | What it means |
| :---- | :---- |
| Not submitted | You have registered but have not sent your documents. |
| Pending Verification | You have sent your documents. An admin has not finished reviewing them. |
| Verified | Your account is approved. You can now submit listings. |
| Verification Rejected | An admin could not approve your documents. You can see the reason. |

**If your verification is rejected**

* You will see the reason the admin gave. For example, an authorization letter that does not name you.

* You cannot post a listing until you are verified. If you try, you will see: "Your account verification was rejected. Update your documents and submit again".

* Fix the problem and submit a new set of documents. Your status goes back to Pending Verification and an admin reviews it again.

* An account that is already Verified cannot submit documents again.

### Step 3: Submit a property

Only a verified account can do this. Verification does not approve your property. It only lets you submit one.

| Information | Required? | Notes |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Title | Yes |  |
| Description | Yes |  |
| Rent price | Yes | The yearly rent |
| State, LGA, and address | Yes | Where the property is |
| Property type | No | Choose one: single room, self-contained room and parlour, one bedroom, two bedroom, or shared apartment. If you do not choose, it is saved as self-contained. There is no separate field for the number of bedrooms. |
| Caution fee | No | If there is one. If you leave it out, it is saved as 0\. |
| Agency fee | No | If there is one. If you leave it out, it is saved as 0\. |
| Distance to PPA | No | Write it honestly, for example "approximately 10 minutes by bus from the PPA". It is a description, not a GPS measurement. |
| Property information | No | For example borehole water, prepaid meter, fenced gate |
| Contact phone and WhatsApp | No | Shown to Corps Members if you provide them |
| Photos | No | Up to three photos. Size and format limits: pending confirmation. |

**Documents:** You send your identity and proof of authority once, in Step 2\. You do not attach documents again for each listing. A verified account can submit many listings, and each listing is approved separately.

When you submit, your listing is saved as a Pending Admin Review. Corps Members cannot see it yet.

### Step 4: Admin reviews your listing

Every listing needs admin approval before it is published. No listing goes live because of your action alone. Admins approve or reject listings. They do not send a listing back for editing.

| Listing status | What it means | What you do |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Pending Admin Review | You submitted it and it is waiting for an admin. | Nothing. Wait for the result. |
| Approved | It is published and visible to Corps Members. | Nothing. |
| Rejected | It was not approved for publication. You can see the admin's reason. | Correct the details and submit a new listing. There is no appeal process in this release. |
| Hidden | An approved listing was removed from public view by an admin, for example after a confirmed scam report. | Contact support. Pending confirmation of the contact channel. |

**After your listing is published**

* If you edit an approved listing, it goes back to Pending Admin Review and disappears from public view until an admin approves it again.

* If you delete a listing, it is archived.

* Corps Members can report a listing. If an admin confirms a scam report, the listing is hidden.

## What NYSC Connect does not do

This is a controlled way to add accommodation information. It is not a property marketplace. In this release there is no booking, no payment or escrow, no chat or messaging, no tenant applications, no inspection scheduling, no negotiation, no ratings or reviews, no featured or paid listings, and no commission. You must not charge any inspection or mobilization fee.

**If something goes wrong**

| Message you may see | What it means | What to do |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Submit identity and authority documents before posting a listing | You have not sent your verification documents. | Complete Step 2\. |
| Your account verification is still pending admin review | An admin has not finished checking your documents. | Wait for the result. |
| Your account verification was rejected. Update your documents and submit again | Your documents were not approved. | Fix them and submit again. |
| Please upload at least one image | You started an image upload without choosing a file. | Choose an image and try again. |
| My upload is slow | On a slow connection an upload can take up to a minute. The system tries once more if it times out. | Wait before trying again. |
| I cannot see my listing on the public pages | It is probably Pending Admin Review or Rejected. | Check its status in your listings. |
