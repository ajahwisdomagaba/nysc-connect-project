# NYSC Connect: Guide for Corps Members

*Version 1.0 (MVP)*

## About this guide

NYSC Connect helps you settle into a new State or LGA. You can find accommodation, read local information, save what you like, and report anything that looks wrong. This guide shows you how, step by step.

*Screen and button names in the app may differ slightly from the words used here. This guide will be checked against the final screens.*

*In the designs, the web menu shows Home, Accommodation, Local Guide, Saved/Favorites, and Essential Services. On mobile, the bottom tabs are Home, Explore, Saved, and Guide. Explore opens the accommodation search.*

## Before you start

You will need your name, phone number, email address, and a password. You also need to know the State and LGA you have been posted to. You do not need to know your PPA yet.

The pilot covers Ikeja and Kosofe in Lagos State, and Ibadan North in Oyo State.

### Step 1: Create your account

1\.  Enter your name, phone number, email address, and password. Your phone number must have at least 10 characters. Your phone number and email must not already be registered.

2\.  Choose your State and LGA.

3\.  Add your PPA, or skip it. You can add it later.

### Step 2: Log in

Log in with your phone number and password. Your email address is not used to log in. Your login lasts for a limited time. If you are logged out, log in again.

### Step 3: Set or change your location

Your State and LGA decide what you see. You can change your State, LGA, or PPA after you register.

Adding your PPA lets listings show how far they are from it. This distance is a short description supplied with the listing, for example "approximately 7 minutes by bus". It is not a GPS measurement.

### Step 4: Your Home screen

Home shows accommodation and local information for your State and LGA. From Home, go to Accommodation or to the Local Guide.

### Step 5: Find accommodation

### Browse and filter

1\.  Open Accommodation to see listings for your location.

2\.  Filter by price range.

3\.  Sort by newest, lowest price, or highest price.

### Read a listing

Open a listing to see:

* Property type, annual rent, caution fee, and agency fee (if any)

* Address and distance to your PPA

* Photos (up to three) and property information

* The source of the information and the date it was last updated

* Contact details, if the listing includes them

### Check how much to trust it

* Source tells you where the information came from.

* The last update tells you how recent it is. If a listing has not been updated for 30 days, it shows "may be outdated".

* Only listings approved by an admin are shown. Approval is a content check. It does not prove that every detail is correct.

* Listings do not show a verified badge. Use the source and the last-updated date to judge a listing.

If you see "No accommodation listings found for this location yet", there are no approved listings for your LGA at the moment. Nothing is broken.

### Step 6: Find local information

The Local Guide has six categories: Transport, PPA Information, Health, Security and Safety, Food, and Essential Services.

1\.  Log in. You need to be logged in to use the Local Guide.

2\.  Open the Local Guide and choose a category.

3\.  Open an entry for your State and LGA to read it.

If you see that information for your LGA is not available yet, nothing has been added for that place so far. This is expected, because content is added location by location.

### Step 7: Save things for later

1\.  Choose Save on a listing or a Local Guide entry.

2\.  Open Saved to see everything you saved. You can remove an item from there.

Saved items are still there after you log in again.

### Step 8: Report something that looks wrong

You can report any listing or Local Guide entry.

1\.  Choose a Report.

2\.  Pick a reason: Outdated, Incorrect, Misleading, or Suspicious.

3\.  Add details if you want to, then submit.

You will see a confirmation. Your report goes to an admin, who reviews it and decides what to do. A confirmed scam report hides the listing.

Use Suspicious if something feels like a scam, for example if someone asks you for money before you have seen the property.

## Staying safe when you look for a place

* Check the source and the last updated date on every listing.

* Confirm the details yourself before you make any payment.

* Landlords and agents who submit listings through NYSC Connect agree not to charge any inspection or mobilization fee. If anyone asks you for one, report the listing.

* NYSC Connect does not handle booking or payment. Never pay through a link or account that someone sends you in the app.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| A network error, or the app will not load | The app cannot reach the server. | Check your internet connection and try again. On the web app, an offline banner appears and screens show a Retry button. The app also tries again when your connection returns. |
| Invalid credentials | Your phone number or password is wrong. | Check both and try again. |
| Please provide phone number and password | A field is empty. | Fill in both fields. |
| You are logged out | Your login time ran out. | Log in again. On the web app, you are taken back to the onboarding screen. |
| No accommodation listings found for this location yet | There are no approved listings for your LGA. | Try another price range, or check back later. |
| Nothing saved yet | You have not saved anything. | Save a listing or a Local Guide entry and it will appear here. |
| Something went wrong. We couldn't load this information. | The app could not load the screen. | Choose Retry. If it keeps happening, check your connection. |
| Information for your LGA is not available yet | Nothing has been added to the Local Guide for your LGA. | Check back later. |
| I cannot register | A field is missing, or your phone number or email is already registered. | Check every field and try again. |

## What is not in this release

There is no booking, payment, chat or messaging, marketplace, or ratings and reviews in this release. Landlords and agents are verified and their listings are approved by an admin before you see them.
